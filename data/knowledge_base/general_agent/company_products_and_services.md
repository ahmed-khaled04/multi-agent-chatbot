# Synthetic Bank Products and Services

Synthetic Bank demonstrates a digital personal-banking service. Availability
can depend on country, account status, identity verification, and product
eligibility.

## Accounts

The bank supports personal checking and savings accounts in eligible
currencies. Customers can view masked account details, balances, and account
status through authenticated services.

## Cards

Supported card forms include physical, virtual, and disposable cards. Card
services cover delivery, activation, status, security, contactless use, and
eligible digital wallets. Specific card details must be checked through an
authorized customer-data tool.

## Payments and cash withdrawals

The service represents card payments, cash withdrawals, direct debits,
refunds, and disputes. Transaction status and outcomes must be checked through
authorized transaction tools.

## Transfers and top-ups

Customers can make eligible bank transfers and add money through supported
top-up methods. General policies describe timing, limits, fees, and
verification, while a specific transfer or top-up must be inspected through an
authorized tool.

## Currencies and exchange

The standard demonstration currency set includes EGP, USD, JOD, EUR, and GBP.
Current availability and exchange rates must be checked through authorized
currency tools because rates and supported features can change.

## What support can do

Support can explain general policies, inspect customer-owned records through
authorized tools, and submit supported requests after confirmation. Support
cannot bypass identity checks, reveal authentication secrets, guarantee a
refund or dispute outcome, or claim an action succeeded without tool
confirmation.
