# Synthetic Bank Company Overview

## Company name

The company is **Synthetic Bank**. It is a fictional digital bank created for
this banking-support application and its demonstration data. It is not a real
financial institution and does not provide real-world banking services.

## Foundation and headquarters

Synthetic Bank was founded in **2024** as a digital-first banking project. Its
fictional headquarters are in **Cairo, Egypt**, with services designed for
customers in supported markets, including Egypt and Jordan.

## Mission

Synthetic Bank's mission is to make everyday banking simple, understandable,
and secure through clear digital experiences. Its support principles are:

- Explain banking information in plain language.
- Protect customer privacy and authentication secrets.
- Clearly distinguish general policy from customer-specific account facts.
- Require confirmation before actions that move money or change customer data.
- Avoid promising outcomes that have not been confirmed by an authorized
  system.

## Operating model

Synthetic Bank is digital-first. Customers primarily manage accounts, cards,
payments, transfers, verification, and support through authenticated digital
channels. Some cash or cheque services may be available through supported
partners rather than bank branches.

## Important clarification

All company details, policies, customers, accounts, transactions, currencies,
fees, and limits in this project are synthetic. They exist for software
development, testing, education, and demonstration only.
