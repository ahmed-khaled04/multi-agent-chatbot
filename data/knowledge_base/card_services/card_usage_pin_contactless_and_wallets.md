# Card Usage, PIN, Contactless, and Digital Wallets

This policy covers general card-use problems. Check the specific card's status
before attributing a problem to activation, freezing, blocking, or expiration.
For a declined or pending payment, inspect the transaction through the payments
tools instead of guessing from the card status alone.

## PIN safety and failed PIN attempts

The customer must never disclose their PIN to support. A PIN should be viewed
or changed only through an authenticated bank channel or supported cash
machine. Support cannot retrieve or state a customer's PIN.

Repeated incorrect PIN attempts can temporarily restrict PIN transactions. The
customer should stop retrying and follow the authenticated application's PIN
recovery instructions. A blocked or expired card cannot be restored by changing
the PIN.

## Contactless payments

Contactless works only when it is enabled for the card and supported by the
merchant terminal. A chip-and-PIN transaction may periodically be required to
confirm possession of the card or reset contactless security limits.

If contactless fails:

1. Check that the card is active and contactless is enabled.
2. Try inserting the card and completing chip-and-PIN verification.
3. Try another supported terminal if it is safe to do so.
4. If chip payments also fail, stop repeated attempts and contact card support.

## Card acceptance

Card acceptance depends on the merchant, network, card form, country, and
transaction type. Offline terminals, deposits, subscriptions, gambling,
cash-like transactions, and delayed charges may not support virtual or
disposable cards. Merchant acceptance cannot be guaranteed.

## Apple Pay and Google Pay

An eligible active card can be added to a supported digital wallet when the
wallet service, device, card network, and customer's country are supported.
Adding a card may require authentication in the bank application.

If wallet verification fails, the customer should confirm that the card is
active, the device is secure and up to date, and the wallet region matches a
supported country. Removing a card from a digital wallet does not close or
block the underlying bank card.

If a phone containing a digital wallet is lost or stolen, the customer should
secure the device account, remove or suspend wallet access remotely when
possible, and contact the bank if the underlying card may be compromised.

## Spare and replacement cards

Availability of spare physical or virtual cards depends on the customer's
account, verification status, and country. Fees and delivery estimates must be
shown in the authenticated application before the customer confirms an order.
