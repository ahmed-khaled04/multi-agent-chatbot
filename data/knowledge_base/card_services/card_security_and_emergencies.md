# Lost, Stolen, Compromised, and Retained Cards

Card-security reports are urgent. Inspect the customer's cards when needed and
show only masked card details. Blocking a card changes customer data and
requires the customer's explicit confirmation before the blocking tool is
called.

## Lost or stolen card

The customer should stop using the missing card and block it immediately in the
authenticated application. If they explicitly ask support to block it, confirm
which masked card they mean before using the blocking tool.

After blocking, the card cannot be used for new purchases or withdrawals. Some
previously authorized or offline transactions can still appear later. Blocking
a card does not automatically dispute transactions and does not close the
linked account.

If the customer later finds a card that was permanently blocked, they must not
attempt to use it. A replacement must be requested through an authenticated
channel.

## Compromised card details

Treat a card as compromised when its full details, PIN, CVV, or authentication
codes may have been exposed, even if the physical card is still present. The
customer should block the card, review recent transactions, and report any
unrecognized payments through the payments and disputes process.

Support must never ask the customer to send photographs of the card or reveal
the full card number, CVV, PIN, passcode, or one-time code.

## Card retained by a cash machine

The customer should not attempt to force open or damage the cash machine. If
the machine belongs to the bank and is at an attended location, staff may
explain the local retrieval procedure after identity verification. Retrieval
is not guaranteed.

For a third-party cash machine, or when immediate retrieval is unavailable, the
safest action is to block the card and request a replacement. If cash was
debited or the withdrawal status is unclear, inspect the transaction through
the payments tools.

## Frozen versus blocked

- **Frozen:** Use is temporarily restricted. The customer may need an
  authenticated card-control or support process to restore it.
- **Blocked:** The card is permanently disabled and cannot be restored through
  chat.
- **Expired:** The validity period ended and a replacement is required.

Do not tell a customer that a card has been secured until an authorized tool or
the application confirms the new status.

## Emergency escalation

Escalate immediately when:

- The customer sees an unrecognized card transaction.
- The customer is being pressured to reveal an authentication code.
- A stolen phone also provides access to the banking application.
- Blocking fails or the card status remains active after a confirmed block.
- The customer is abroad and has no safe way to access funds.
