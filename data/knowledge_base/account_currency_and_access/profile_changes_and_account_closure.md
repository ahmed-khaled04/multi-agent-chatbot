# Personal Detail Changes and Account Closure

Profile changes and account-closure requests affect customer data. Obtain
explicit confirmation immediately before calling a tool that creates either
request. A submitted request is not the same as a completed change or closure.

## Personal detail changes

Customers can request changes to:

- Full name
- Email address
- Country
- Phone number
- Preferred language

Changes are submitted for review. The standard request statuses are requested,
applied, rejected, and cancelled. Name, country, phone, or email changes may
require identity or address evidence through a secure authenticated channel.

Support must not claim the profile changed merely because a request was
created. The customer should continue using their current verified details
until the application confirms that the update was applied.

## Before closing an account

Before requesting closure, the customer should:

1. Bring the balance to zero or arrange an approved transfer of remaining
   funds.
2. Resolve pending payments, transfers, cash withdrawals, refunds, disputes,
   chargebacks, and fees.
3. Cancel or move direct debits and recurring card payments.
4. Download any statements or records they need.
5. Confirm that linked cards and services are no longer required.

An account can require review before closure when it has pending activity, a
non-zero balance, a security restriction, or a legal or compliance hold.

## Closure request statuses

- **Requested:** The closure request was received.
- **Under review:** The bank is checking whether closure requirements are met.
- **Completed:** The account was closed.
- **Rejected:** The account could not be closed and the reason should be shown.
- **Cancelled:** The closure request was withdrawn before completion.

The account remains open until the request status is completed. Creating a
closure request does not immediately close the account or cancel pending
transactions.

## After closure

A closed account cannot receive or send new payments. Cards linked only to that
account stop working. Regulatory record-retention obligations can continue
after closure, and closure does not erase transaction records that the bank is
required to retain.

## Escalation

Escalate when a customer cannot submit required verification securely, a
profile update remains unresolved beyond its stated review period, or a closure
request is blocked by activity the customer cannot identify or resolve.
