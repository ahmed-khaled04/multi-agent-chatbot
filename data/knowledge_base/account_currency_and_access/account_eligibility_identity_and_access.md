# Account Eligibility, Identity Verification, and Access Recovery

This guide covers general eligibility, identity checks, forgotten passcodes,
and lost phones. Use account tools for a customer's actual accounts and status.
Never infer a customer's verification or account status from this policy.

## General eligibility

Personal accounts are available to eligible adults aged 18 or older in
supported countries. Applicants need a supported mobile number, a unique email
address, and valid identity documentation. Availability remains subject to
identity, residency, sanctions, fraud, and regulatory checks.

Meeting the general requirements does not guarantee approval. Support cannot
bypass eligibility or verification controls.

## Identity verification statuses

- **Unverified:** Required checks have not been completed.
- **Pending:** Information was submitted and is being reviewed.
- **Verified:** Required identity checks were completed.
- **Failed:** The submitted information could not be verified.

Reviews normally complete within one to three business days after all requested
information is received. Complex cases can take longer. Support must not promise
approval or a specific completion time.

Documents must be current, readable, complete, and submitted through the
authenticated application. Customers must not send identity documents through
ordinary chat.

## Source-of-funds verification

The bank may request evidence explaining where money came from. Examples can
include a payslip, bank statement, sale agreement, or other relevant lawful
record. The requested evidence depends on the transaction and customer.

Support cannot advise customers to split transactions, change descriptions, or
otherwise avoid verification.

## Forgotten passcode

The customer should use the application's passcode-recovery flow. Recovery may
require control of the registered phone or email, device verification, and a
fresh identity check. Support cannot view, recover, or reset a passcode through
chat and must never ask the customer to reveal it.

## Lost or stolen phone

The customer should secure the phone and mobile account, use remote device-lock
or erase features when available, and begin account recovery from a trusted
device. If unauthorized access is suspected, escalate through an authenticated
security channel immediately.

Changing phones or phone numbers may trigger additional verification. Existing
cards are not automatically blocked when a phone is lost, so card security must
be handled separately when card or wallet access may also be compromised.
